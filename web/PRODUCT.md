# Aquavelle

The Bangladesh river atlas joins the supplied RiverWatch site records, candidate river links and modelled traces to their exact source runs. It also exposes the Dhaka satellite pilot and links to the distinct dated field measurements. The rainwater workspace combines the Borsha-Bandhu drawings, field references and a seasonal calculator with the original daily calculator.

## Current release · V4, 17 November 2026

Three plain-language tasks now lead the platform: find a recorded water source, check historical river evidence along actual mapped channel connections, and calculate a rainwater reserve with visible shortages. Private source checks, entered measurements and follow-up cases persist in SQLite/D1. The prior response map and research workspace remain secondary. WPdx inventory, original-coordinate chemistry, distinct publisher context, unlocated BASIS quality and velocity-only survey points keep their source limits. The platform does not infer potable water, sustainable groundwater yield, verified flow or pollutant transport. Current setup and methods are in README.md and docs/WATER-PLATFORM-V4-METHODS.md; competition dates below describe earlier context.

## Product Purpose

Turn environmental evidence into feasible actions and checkable execution records. The initial demonstration covers nor'wester disruption, salinity source restriction, and seasonal dry-period stress.

## Operating Context

User confirmed Bangladesh first, a lean 2–5-person adult student team, under USD 1,000 excluding labor, and no confirmed field partner. Deadline target October 25, 2026; official submission window is contradictory and needs organizer clarification.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The legacy continuity scenarios and supplied Dacope assets/hazards are representational. New operational records are signed-in user entries; GPS is actual device-reported telemetry only when explicitly shared, current and labelled. Replay never becomes live GPS. Never infer a safe well, field impact, current official hazard, flood-safe route or independently authenticated lab result. Show source, method and uncertainty for every derived claim. Preserve English/Bangla resident guidance.
