# Competition and stakeholder submission pack

## Project title and summary

**Climate++ — auditable water-resilience decisions for Bangladesh**

Climate++ connects environmental evidence with practical local action. Its prototype combines historical pollution screening and GIS, a validated-input source-investigation model, a trained dissolved-oxygen estimator, and a separate water-continuity simulation for nor'wester disruption, coastal salinity and dry-period stress. Operators can inspect assumptions, compare feasible responses, confirm a demonstration plan, produce resident notices and export checkable evidence records.

The initial customer hypothesis is an institutional water operator or NGO managing a cluster of community assets. Residents receive service information free. The next step is an operator-led shadow pilot to evaluate decision quality, workload, service continuity and environmental outcomes.

## IEEE track fit

Use **Climate Data & Environmental Monitoring**. The evidence chain addresses fragmented environmental information and verifiability: source versions, measurements, explicit objectives, reproducible analysis, decision records and integrity checks. The working AI component estimates measured DO with a chronological test; blockchain is demonstrated on a local EVM. No public-testnet or live operational deployment claim is made.

## Judge questions and defensible answers

**Is this a new pollution index?** It is an explicit partial objective-exceedance statistic. We do not claim a newly validated ecological index. The library implements CCME with eligibility checks, but the default panel does not qualify for a standard CCME score.

**Can you identify the polluting factory?** Not from these datasets alone. We support sampling-location context, measured drain contribution checks and a defensible investigation workflow. Confirmation requires authorized pathway tracing and pollutant-specific evidence.

**What is the AI doing?** A frozen random forest estimates measured DO from other contemporaneous inputs. On 174 later-period test records its MAE is 1.159 mg/L, versus 1.548 for a monthly baseline. Only nine test dates exist; intervals are wide. It is not used to certify safety.

**Why blockchain?** It demonstrates a shared digest registry and correction trail that could support independently held evidence commitments. A signed append-only database may suffice for a single operator. Blockchain does not make an observation true, and the current chain is local development only.

**What real impact have you established?** Software correctness, reproducibility and retrospective/simulation evidence. Field impact and adoption have not yet been demonstrated.

**Why would an operator adopt it?** The hypothesis is reduced coordination burden and more explainable response decisions. We will test that against existing paper, spreadsheet and platform workflows before charging.

**How does it scale?** By repeating a validated operator-cluster workflow with training, local objectives, authorized data integration and support, rather than copying identical assumptions across the country.
