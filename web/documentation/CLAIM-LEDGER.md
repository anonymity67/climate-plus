## Claims not supported

- Proven disease prevention, lives saved, actual water savings, customers served or household travel reduction.
- Certified safe drinking water, automatic legal compliance decisions or a verified current pollution emergency.
- Identification of a specific industrial polluter from proximity, COD, fecal coliform or the current GIS alone.
- A national deployment, government approval, signed pilot partner, paid customer, measured willingness to pay or guaranteed competition success.
- A world-first WQI, source-mixing equation, GIS method, blockchain registry or patentable invention.
- Guaranteed uncertainty coverage, causal ENSO attribution, unseen-basin generalization or reliable real-time forecasting.

## Credible novelty statement

“Climate++ combines existing scientific methods into an auditable Bangladesh operating workflow: explicit data gaps, constrained service planning, source-investigation diagnostics, confirmed notices and reproducible evidence receipts. Its incremental value must be tested against existing operator practice.”
