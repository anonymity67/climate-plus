# Aquavelle in three minutes

Open the local preview using the setup in the project README.

1. **Start where a household starts.** Open [Find water](http://127.0.0.1:5198/?view=water), choose Khulna → Koyra and open a result. Point out the survey date and the verification steps. Then switch to Bangla.
2. **Follow the evidence on the map.** Open [a RiverWatch lead](http://127.0.0.1:5198/?view=rivers&lead=IND-n10026728102) and show the modelled river trace and the nearby-area layer. In the [Dhaka pilot](http://127.0.0.1:5198/?view=rivers&run=dhaka_pilot&lead=IND-n3529135319), open the satellite comparison and its table. The trace helps plan a visit, and the satellite readings compare water appearance on the same image.
3. **Turn a shortage into a plan.** Open [Rainwater](http://127.0.0.1:5198/?view=supply), choose School or shelter and Barind & Char, keep the reduced-rainfall scenario, and calculate. At the preset demand of 2,000 litres per day, the model supplies 23.9% of the November–May need and leaves 322,840 litres to plan through another source. Open the [system drawing](http://127.0.0.1:5198/?view=supply#harvest-drawings) and show where the first runoff is diverted.

The scenario figures depend on the entered demand and supplied rainfall profile. The demonstration uses historical records, modelled traces and estimates. Establish current conditions through local checks and testing before any field decision.
