Third-party code, data and assets keep their own licences. `npm ci` downloads packages with their own licence notices. Preserve `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`, `DATASETS.json`, `public/fonts/OFL.txt`, the OpenStreetMap attribution, and dataset author, version and DOI references when modifying or redistributing.

The Dhaka and BASIS dataset records state CC BY 4.0. Attribute the original authors, link their source and licence, and identify the Climate++ transformations. They do not endorse this project. NASA, NOAA, Gazette and font references keep the source-specific terms described in the attribution and register files. Reference inclusion does not imply a separate licence grant over government publications.

The Dacope boundary GeoPackage in `research/gis/v1/` comes from a source whose licence has not been confirmed. Confirm it before redistributing that file, or remove it from the repository.
