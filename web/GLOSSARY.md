# Aquavelle

## River evidence

**Mapped site** — A facility or waste location recorded in OpenStreetMap. Its point can represent the facility itself, such as a centroid, rather than where water leaves the site.

**River link** — RiverWatch’s candidate association between a mapped site and a HydroRIVERS reach. A reviewed link requires confirmation of the outlet and flow direction.

**Downstream trace** — The section of the encoded river network followed from a candidate link. This is a modelled path. Real flow can change with tides, gates and seasons.

**Proximity area** — The modelled 500 m area beside a downstream trace. Population and facility counts describe proximity. Exposure and contamination require separate evidence.

**Priority lead** — A mapped high-hazard category within 500 m of a linked reach under RiverWatch’s screening rules. Some priority leads stop before a downstream trace can be drawn.

**Satellite pair** — Two readings of water appearance from the same Sentinel-2 scene, taken at an upstream and a downstream comparison point.

**River sample** — A dated, located measurement collected and tested under the historical field study. Its proximity to a trace does not assign the result to the site.

## Rainwater harvesting

**Rainfall profile** — Twelve illustrative regional monthly rainfall totals carried over from the Borsha-Bandhu design archive.

**Shortfall** — The amount of entered demand the tank cannot supply during the selected calculation period.

**First flush** — The initial runoff diverted away from storage. Safe drinking use also depends on suitable treatment, maintenance and testing.
